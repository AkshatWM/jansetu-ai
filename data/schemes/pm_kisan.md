# PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi (पीएम किसान सम्मान निधि)

## Overview
Central sector income support scheme providing ₹6,000 per year in 3 equal instalments of ₹2,000 directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts via DBT. Official portal: pmkisan.gov.in.

## Eligibility
- Landholding farmer families (husband, wife, and minor children) owning cultivable land recorded in UP Bhulekh / Khatauni (खतौनी).
- Mandatory e-KYC and Aadhaar-bank seeding.
- Exclusions: Income tax payers, institutional landholders, serving/retired government employees (except Class IV/Group D), pensioners receiving ₹10,000+ per month, and registered professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs).

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card (आधार कार्ड)
- Land ownership record / Khatauni (खतौनी / भूलेख)
- Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook (बैंक पासबुक)
- Active mobile number for e-KYC OTP

## Required Form Slots
applicant_name, father_or_husband_name, district, address
