# UP Old Age Pension Scheme (वृद्धावस्था पेंशन योजना - SSPY)

## Overview
Monthly financial assistance of ₹1,000 per month (disbursed quarterly as ₹3,000 via DBT) by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh. Official portal: sspy-up.gov.in.

## Eligibility
- Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh (उत्तर प्रदेश का निवासी).
- Minimum age: 60 years or above (उम्र 60 वर्ष या अधिक).
- Annual family income limit (वार्षिक पारिवारिक आय सीमा): Rural (ग्रामीण) up to ₹46,080 per year; Urban (शहरी) up to ₹56,460 per year.
- Must not be receiving any other state/central pension or government service pension.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card (आधार कार्ड)
- Age Proof / Date of Birth Certificate (आयु प्रमाण पत्र)
- Income Certificate issued by Tehsil (आय प्रमाण पत्र)
- Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook (बैंक पासबुक)
- Passport-size photograph

## Required Form Slots
applicant_name, father_or_husband_name, age, annual_income_inr, district, address
