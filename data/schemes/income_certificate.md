# UP Income Certificate (आय प्रमाण पत्र - e-District / eSathi)

## Overview
Official certificate verifying annual family income, issued by the Tehsildar after Lekhpal field verification. Official portal: esathi.up.gov.in. Fee: ₹15 online (₹30 at CSC). Processing time: 7–15 working days (official limit 20 days). Validity: 3 years.

## Eligibility
- Any permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh requiring income verification for pensions, scholarships (UP Scholarship), EWS/OBC-NCL (₹8 lakh limit), or welfare schemes.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card (आधार कार्ड)
- Signed Self-Declaration Form (स्वप्रमाणित घोषणा पत्र)
- Ration Card or Parivar Register Nakal (राशन कार्ड)
- Passport-size photograph
- Income proof (salary slip/Form 16 for salaried, ITR for business, or Bhulekh Khatauni for farmers)

## Required Form Slots
applicant_name, father_or_husband_name, annual_income_inr, district, address
