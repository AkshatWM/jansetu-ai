# UP Caste Certificate (जाति प्रमाण पत्र - e-District / eSathi)

## Overview
Official SC/ST/OBC caste certificate issued by the Tehsildar after Lekhpal verification via esathi.up.gov.in. Fee: ₹15 online (₹30 at CSC). Processing time: 7–20 working days.

## Eligibility
- Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh belonging to a notified Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), or Other Backward Class (OBC) category in UP.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card (आधार कार्ड)
- Signed Self-Declaration Form (स्वप्रमाणित घोषणा पत्र)
- Passport-size photograph
- Residence proof (Ration Card / Voter ID / Electricity Bill)
- Caste verification proof (father's/family caste certificate or Gram Pradhan / Ward Parshad certification)

## Required Form Slots
applicant_name, father_or_husband_name, district, address
