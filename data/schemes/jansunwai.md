# UP Jansunwai - IGRS Grievance Portal (जनसुनवाई शिकायत पोर्टल)

## Overview
Integrated Grievance Redressal System (IGRS) by the Government of Uttar Pradesh (jansunwai.up.nic.in) for filing and tracking public grievances free of cost.

## Eligibility
- Any citizen of Uttar Pradesh facing issues with government services (electricity, ration card, pension delay, revenue/land disputes, water supply, municipal services, police).
- Exclusions: Sub-judice matters (pending in court), RTI applications, and general suggestions.

## Required Documents
- Mobile number for OTP verification
- Concise description of the grievance (शिकायत का विवरण)
- Optional supporting evidence (photo/PDF of bills, prior application, or notice)

## Required Form Slots
applicant_name, district, address, grievance_summary
